4.9 Scope & Purpose 
 
  In India, the incidence of Disaster is an annual and recurring 
phenomenon.  The Nilgiris hills rises abruptly from the surrounding plains 
to an elevation of 1370 m above MSL and it is surrounded by the 
Coimbatore plains in the southeast, Bhavani plains in the northeast, Moyar 
Valley in the North and Gudalur plateau in the Northwest.  The 
Charncokite group of rocks with the enclaves of Sathyamangalam Schist 
complex gets exposed in The Nilgiris District.  The landslide incidences are 
high to moderate in Western Ghats and The Nilgiris.  The landslide hazard 
location atlas of India published by Building Materials and Technology 
Promotion Council (BMTPC), Government of India reveals that the Nilgiris 
District of Tamil Nadu State is one of the severe to very high landslide 
hazard prone areas of India.  In order to reduce the enormous destructive 
potential of the disasters and to minimise the consequential loses, it is 
necessary that hazard must first be recognized, then analyzed and then an 
appropriate strategy to be developed. 
 
4.10 Expected Outcome: 
  The District is categorized under severe to very high landslide prone 
area.  This indicates, the area is well known as perennial threat to life and 
property.  The main challenge is to have an optimum balance between 
protecting the indigenous ecosystem and increasing urbanization.  Nature 
with its bounty of resource and biodiversity, have also bestowed risk areas 
also. The same is more complicated by the human actions and systems 
focusing on the present systems and circumstances and it becomes an 
impossible task on human aura to avert a natural fury.  But it is well 
within the limit to reduce the impact and effect of disaster.  The desired 
outcome would be to develop a situation to strike a balance of impact on 
life and property by a disaster.

4.19 Do’s and Don’ts during all possible Hazards  
 
4.19.1 Landslide 
 
• Before Landslide 
 
• Safety Tips 
 
➢ Do ground assessment of your property with the help of a 
geotechnical expert and take preventive steps 
➢ Be familiar with the land around your house 
➢ Be aware of areas affected from landslides in the past, and be 
cautious 
➢ Plan atleast two evacuation routes since roads may be blocked or 
closed / prepare an emergency evacuation plan. 
 
• During Landslide 
 
• Safety Tips 
 
➢ If inside a building, stay inside 
➢ If outdoor try and get out of the path of the landslide.  Run to the 
nearest high ground in a direction away from the path of the 
landslide. 
➢ If escape is not possible, curl into a tight ball and protect your head. 
 
• After a Landslide
